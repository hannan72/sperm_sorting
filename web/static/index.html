3 The decision rule

=
Exactly 60% REJECTS. The comparison is a strict >. 15/25 is not acceptable; 16/25 is. The engine compares exact rationals, so the boundary does not depend on floating-point rounding.
⚡
FIELD_ON is the rejection. Energising the magnet diverts the segment to the waste channel. FIELD_OFF is the pass-through to collection, and is also the safe state used when no decision could be made. Reading FIELD_ON as “good” inverts the product.
